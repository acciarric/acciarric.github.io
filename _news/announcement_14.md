I am delighted to announce [La scienza cambia la vita](https://www.ritrattidimatematiche.unimore.it/mostra-e-scuole/la-scienza-cambia-la-vita/) ("Science changes lives"), an event for schools organized within the 2026 Public Engagement project *Officine STEM*, which grew out of [Ritratti di Matematiche dal mondo](https://www.ritrattidimatematiche.unimore.it/).

Six speakers from school, university, industry and science communication will tell how science has changed their lives, and how it can change ours too: Federico Benuzzi (teacher, science communicator and actor), Olindo Corradini (UNIMORE, theoretical physics), Rita Fioresi (UniBo, geometry), Marcello Pellacani (entrepreneur), Lorenzo Pianelli (teacher and content creator) and Barbara Zardin (UNIMORE, energy systems). After the talks, students will have the chance to meet and talk with the speakers in an informal dialogue session.

When: Tuesday, December 15, 2026, from 9:30 AM to 12:30 PM

Where: Room M1.1, first floor of the Mathematics Building, Dipartimento di Scienze Fisiche, Informatiche e Matematiche - UNIMORE, Via Campi 213/b, Modena.

Program and registration details are available at [the event page](https://www.ritrattidimatematiche.unimore.it/mostra-e-scuole/la-scienza-cambia-la-vita/).
